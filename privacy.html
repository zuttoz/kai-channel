Privacy policy

Kai Channel is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uploads videos to, and reads statistics from, the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services.

It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else. Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Kai Channel uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
